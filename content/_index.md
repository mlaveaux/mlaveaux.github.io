Welcome to my personal website!

I am currently employed as a [scientific
programmer](https://research.tue.nl/en/persons/maurice-laveaux/) at the
Eindhoven University of Technology, within the Formal System Analysis (FSA)
group of the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science.

My main responsibility is the maintenance and development of new algorithms for
our long-running model checking tool called [mCRL2](https://www.mcrl2.org/).
Furthermore, I am supporting my colleagues with the implementation of their
theoretical research and preparation of artifacts. Occasionally, I am also
involved in the research itself.

As part of this employment I am actively involved in several research and
industrial projects:

 - [STORM_SAFE](https://www.interregnorthsea.eu/stormsafe).
 - [Future Network Services](https://futurenetworkservices.nl/en/) with industrial partner [CORDIS Automation Bv](https://www.cordis-suite.com/).
 - [Cynergie4Mie](https://cynergy4mie.eu/).

Occasionally I am also involved in teaching activities, such as supervising
(master) students and working as teaching assistant for the Software
Specification course.

My full CV can be found [here](cv/cv.md).

My main interests are in the field of formal methods, and the design and
implementation of practical algorithms. However, I also believe that

Our mCRL2 toolset is written in C++, which is my main expertise, but I am also

# Previous work

In 2022 I successfully defended my PhD thesis called "Accelerated Verification of
Concurrent Systems" with prof.dr.ir. Tim A.C. Willemse and prof.dr.ir. Jan Friso
Groote. The thesis can be found
[here](https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/accelerated-verification-of-concurrent-systems).
This research was funded as part of a Dutch Research Council (NWO) TOP C1 grant
program under project number 612.001.751 (Accelerated Verification and Verified
Acceleration) together with colleagues dr. Jan Martens and dr. Tom Franken.
